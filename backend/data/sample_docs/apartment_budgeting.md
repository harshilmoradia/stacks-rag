# Budgeting for a First Apartment

## Rent
A common guideline is to spend no more than 30% of your gross monthly
income on rent. Going above that makes it harder to cover savings and
unexpected expenses.

## Security Deposit
Expect to pay one to two months' rent as a security deposit, due
before move-in along with the first month's rent.

## Renters Insurance
Renters insurance is usually required by landlords and typically costs
$15-30/month. It covers your belongings and liability, since the
landlord's insurance only covers the building itself.

## Hidden Costs
Budget for a rental application fee (often $30-75, non-refundable),
moving costs (truck rental or movers), and setup fees for utilities
and internet that aren't included in rent.

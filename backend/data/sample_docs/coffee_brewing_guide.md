# Home Coffee Brewing Guide

## Water Temperature
Use water between 195°F and 205°F. Boiling water (212°F) will scorch
the grounds and produce a bitter cup. If you don't have a thermometer,
let boiled water rest for 30 seconds before pouring.

## Grind Size
Pour-over methods need a medium grind, similar to sea salt. French
press needs a coarse grind, similar to breadcrumbs. A grind that's too
fine for your method will over-extract and taste bitter; too coarse
will under-extract and taste sour or weak.

## Ratio
Use a ratio of 1:16, coffee to water by weight. For a single 300ml cup,
that's about 19 grams of coffee.

## Freshness
Coffee is best used within two weeks of its roast date, and goes stale
within a month of grinding. Store beans whole and grind right before
brewing when possible.

## Storage
Keep beans in an airtight container, away from light and heat. Don't
store coffee in the refrigerator — condensation degrades the beans
faster than room-temperature storage.
